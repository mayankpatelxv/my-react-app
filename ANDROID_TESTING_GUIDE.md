## ✅ Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Clear Browser Cache on Android

#### For Chrome on Android:
1. Open **Chrome** browser
2. Tap the **three dots** (⋮) in top-right corner
3. Tap **Settings**
4. Tap **Privacy and security**
5. Tap **Clear browsing data**
6. Select **"Cached images and files"** (check the box)
7. Tap **"Clear data"** button
8. Wait for confirmation

#### For Samsung Internet:
1. Open **Samsung Internet** browser
2. Tap the **three lines** (≡) menu
3. Tap **Settings**
4. Tap **Privacy and security**
5. Tap **Delete browsing data**
6. Select **"Cached images and files"**
7. Tap **Delete**
